Timed scope: one direct call to WheelSpeedFusion_Task5ms.
Initialization and scenario setup occur before timing.

Nominal scenario (typical):
- Each five-element period history is strictly increasing.
- MedianOfFiveU16 therefore performs no element-shift iterations in its while body.

Boundary scenario:
- All five period values for a wheel are equal to 1100.
- The insertion-sort while comparison is evaluated but its body is not entered.

Upper-path scenario:
- Each five-element period history is strictly decreasing (reverse sorted).
- Analyze the maximum insertion-sort shifts allowed by five elements.
- Period and pulse values remain nonzero, so PeriodToSpeedQ8 division executes.
- Vehicle speed is greater than 256, so EvaluateWheelSlip division executes.

Do not treat the ascending nominal scenario as covering the reverse-sorted path.
