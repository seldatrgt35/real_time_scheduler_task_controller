Timed scope: one direct call to BatteryContactorSupervisor_Task10ms.
Initialization and scenario setup occur before timing.

Nominal scenario (typical):
- Six channels use the values installed by AutomotiveHoldoutV2_Initialize.
- Contactor state is channel_index & 3.
- Channel 4 command is zero; other commands are one.

Boundary scenario:
- abs(pack_voltage - bus_voltage) is exactly 85 for every channel.
- Coil current alternates between exactly 90 and exactly 680.

Upper-path scenario to analyze:
- abs(pack_voltage - bus_voltage) is 300 for every channel.
- Coil current alternates between 70 and 720.
- Commands alternate zero/one and states cycle through 1, 2, 3.
- Report the state/current/command combination producing the selected path.
